More About Me

A Few Experiences Worth Sharing

I enjoy travel and exploring novel cultures, cuisine, and meeting people from different backgrounds. Seeing new places reminds me how many different ways there are to live and solve problems, and I always come back with ideas I wouldn't have had otherwise.

I have travelled to five out of seven continents. I'm currently studying abroad at NYU Florence, immersing myself in the rich history in Renaissance that the city holds. Outside of class, I am constantly exploring museums, monuments, and the scenery of Florence and nearby cities.

One place I keep returning to is the Uffizi Gallery, home to some of the most important Renaissance paintings in the world.
