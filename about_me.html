About Me

My Background

Hi, I'm Chelsea Yang. I'm currently studying Interactive Media Art at New York University. I'm an aspiring creative technologist, and enjoys creating different mediums of art in my free time. Being in New York, you can always find me exploring galleries and the latest exhibition.

This is one of my favorite spots in New York — the High Line.

My Interests

I'm interested in web development, design, and the ways software shapes everyday life. I enjoy reading, photography, and a good cup of coffee while I work. I'm currently exploring how good user interfaces make complex things feel simple.

I keep up with web technologies through the MDN Web Docs, a great resource for anyone learning to build for the web.
